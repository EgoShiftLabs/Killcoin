# Agency Character Visual Standard — Vanguard (Nov 2026)

## Younger frontline operatives

- BLACKOUT: Black male field operative in early 30s, athletic, cropped natural hair, precise short beard, luxury black tactical streetwear with gold highlights. Calm authority; no gray hair.
- VESPER: Black woman in late 20s, deliberately silver-white pixie hairstyle (style, not age), hoop earrings, sleek glossy black tactical styling.
- SABLE: Young woman in late 20s with medium tan complexion, expressive dark eyes, black headscarf with ornate gold accents and black tactical coat with crimson interior.

Palette: midnight black, carbon, crimson and brass gold. Wet Detroit-inspired streets, glass penthouses, neon industrial environments. Characters should look modern and confident.

### Legacy originals

The older visual versions of the three leads are preserved in assets/operatives/founders-archive/ as historical concept artwork. Their future fictional names and storyline affiliations remain unassigned; do not assert they are the same people at different ages.

### Paths

The default current portraits retain canonical asset paths in assets/operatives/: blackout.webp, vesper.webp, sable.webp. The existing roster, recruitment cards and stories can reuse them without changing JavaScript.
